# 🖼️ 3D Photo Gallery

> Your photos, framed on the walls of a virtual gallery hall. Walk through in 3D, tap a photo to fly up close, and enjoy your own private exhibition.

---

## ✨ About

**3D Photo Gallery** turns your photos into a museum-style exhibition. Upload your pictures and they appear in golden frames on the walls of a 3D hall — with spotlights, benches and caption plates under every frame, just like a real gallery. Your photos stay saved, so the exhibition is ready whenever you come back.

## 🚀 Features

- 🏛️ **A virtual 3D hall** — deep-green walls, wooden floor, brass frames and spotlights
- 🖼️ **16 frames** — fill the hall with your favourite photos
- ⬆️ **Easy upload** — JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 10 MB, several at once
- ✏️ **Caption plates** — every photo gets its own caption under the frame
- 👀 **Look around freely** — drag to explore the hall
- 🎯 **Fly to a photo** — tap any photo, or use Previous / Next, and the camera glides to it
- ▶️ **Auto tour** — sit back and let the gallery show itself
- 🗂️ **Manage photos** — edit captions or remove photos anytime
- 💾 **Saved forever** — uploads persist in the gallery's own storage

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Tap **+ Upload a photo** and pick pictures from your device.
2. They appear framed on the walls — drag to look around the hall.
3. Tap a photo to view it up close, or start the Auto tour.
